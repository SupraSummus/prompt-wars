How it works

  1. Write a spell

    Up to {{ max_spell_length }} characters of anything: instructions, tricks, poems, emoji, any language. Whatever gets a model to repeat you and ignore the other text.

  2. It battles on its own

    Every battle moves your spell's rating. Come back to see whom it beat, and what the model said.

Good to know

Do I need an account?

No. Without one, this browser remembers your spells for a while, and signing up later moves them into your account. An account keeps them on any device and lets you challenge other spells to duels.

Does the model pick the winner?

No. The model isn't told there's a game: it just answers the glued text. The score is counted afterwards, from how much of each spell the reply repeats, so arguing that you should win helps only as far as the model repeats it.

Who can read my spell?

Its text is shown to you, and to anyone who writes exactly the same text. Your opponents read the model's replies in their battles against you, and a reply often echoes your spell, so don't put anything private in one. See the data policy.
