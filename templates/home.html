  1. Write a prompt

    Up to {{ max_warrior_length }} characters of anything: instructions, tricks, poems, emoji, any language. Whatever gets a model to repeat you and ignore the other text.

Good to know

Do I need an account?

No. Without one, this browser remembers your prompts for a while, and signing up later moves them into your account. An account keeps them on any device and lets you challenge other prompts to duels.

Does the model pick the winner?

No. The model isn't told there's a game: it just answers the glued text. The score is counted afterwards, from how much of each prompt the reply repeats, so arguing that you should win helps only as far as the model repeats it.

Who can read my prompt?

It is shown to you, and to anyone who writes exactly the same text. Your opponents read the model's replies in their battles against you, and a reply often echoes your prompt, so don't put anything private in one. See the data policy.
