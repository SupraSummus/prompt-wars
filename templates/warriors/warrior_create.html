Your spell is glued to an opponent's, with no space between, and sent to the model, once in each order. The more of your spell's characters the reply repeats, in order, the more of the game you win.

  • Ask for your text back, word for word.
  • Tell the model to ignore whatever comes before or after your spell.
  • Keep it short: a short spell is easier to repeat whole.
  • Characters your opponent is unlikely to use, like emoji or another script, can only count for you.
