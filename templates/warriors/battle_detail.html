How to read the scores
Score
A warrior's share of one game. The two warriors' shares in a game add up to 100%.
Battle score
The mean of a warrior's scores over the games, once every game is resolved.
Result similarity
How closely the LLM's result resembles a warrior's prompt.
Warriors similarity
How alike the two warriors are to begin with.
Cooperation score
High when both prompts survive into the result in balance, and the prompts are distinct.

Each algorithm scores the same games its own way.
