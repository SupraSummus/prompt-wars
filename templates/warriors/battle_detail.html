How to read the scores
Score
A prompt's share of one game. The two prompts' shares in a game add up to 100%.
Battle score
The mean of a prompt's scores over the games, once every game is resolved.
Reply similarity
How closely the model's reply resembles a prompt.
Prompt similarity
How alike the two prompts are to begin with.
Cooperation score
High when both prompts survive into the reply in balance, and the prompts are distinct.

Each algorithm scores the same games its own way.
